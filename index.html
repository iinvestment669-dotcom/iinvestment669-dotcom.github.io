About Our Farm

From Port Harcourt to Your Pot

Prime Snail Farm NG started with a passion for healthy protein. We specialize in African Giant Snails (GALS) raised in a clean, controlled environment. Our snails are fed with natural leaves, fruits and formulated feed - no chemicals. Whether you need 20 pieces for soup or 1000 pieces for your restaurant or for breeding, we have you covered.
